# Pricing Policy

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA** — This document is entirely fictional and was written
> for a software demonstration. It is not a real credit policy, not financial
> advice, and must not be used to make real lending decisions.

## Base interest rates (per annum, reducing balance)

| Product       | Base rate |
|---------------|-----------|
| Personal Loan | 11.0%     |
| Home Loan     | 8.5%      |
| Auto Loan     | 9.5%      |
| Business Loan | 12.0%     |

## Risk-based adjustments

The final offered rate is the product base rate plus an adjustment driven by
the application's risk band:

| Risk band | Adjustment |
|-----------|------------|
| Low       | +0.0 pp    |
| Medium    | +1.5 pp    |
| High      | +3.0 pp    |

Risk bands are derived from the internal scorecard (300–900 scale):
low = 750+, medium = 650–749, high = below 650.

## Fees

- Processing fee: 1.0% of the loan amount, capped at INR 25,000.
- The EMI quoted to the applicant must use the final offered rate.
