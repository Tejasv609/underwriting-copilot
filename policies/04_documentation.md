# Documentation Checklist

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA** — This document is entirely fictional and was written
> for a software demonstration. It is not a real credit policy, not financial
> advice, and must not be used to make real lending decisions.

## Required documents by product

### All products
- Identity proof (PAN or Aadhaar)
- Address proof
- Income proof (3 months' payslips or latest ITR)
- Bank statements (6 months)
- Employment proof (offer letter or business registration)

### Secured products (Home Loan, Auto Loan) — additionally
- Collateral valuation report
- Property / vehicle ownership documents

## Handling of missing documents

- Missing income proof or identity proof is treated as a critical gap: the
  application cannot proceed until the applicant provides it.
- Missing any other single document routes the application to referral with a
  condition to collect the document before disbursal.
- Missing two or more non-critical documents routes the application to referral.
